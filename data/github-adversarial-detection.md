## Project Summary

A security-focused project that investigates adversarial attacks hidden within documents, images, and structured content, aiming to identify potentially malicious payloads before they reach AI systems.

## Problem It Solves

Modern AI systems are vulnerable to prompt injections, hidden instructions, and adversarial content embedded in files.

# ZapMail

## Repository

https://github.com/anuragdaksh7/zap-frontend

## Project Summary

ZapMail is an event-driven email automation platform designed to trigger and manage personalized email workflows through developer-friendly interfaces and programmatic integrations.

## Problem It Solves

Existing email automation platforms are often expensive, complex, and targeted toward enterprise users. ZapMail aims to provide a lightweight alternative for developers.

## Tech Stack

### Messaging

### Email Infrastructure

* SMTP integrations
* Email APIs
